TubePilot

TubePilot is a personal desktop app that helps its owner run their own YouTube channels. It reads channel and video statistics, prepares new videos for review, and uploads them only after the owner approves each one.

What it does

  • Shows subscribers, views and likes for the owner's own channels.
  • Prepares titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails for new videos.
  • Uploads approved videos and sets their publishing schedule.

TubePilot runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public.
