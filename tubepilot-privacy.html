Privacy Policy for TubePilot

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

TubePilot ("the app") is a personal desktop app that manages its owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. TubePilot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

After you sign in with Google and grant access, the app reads your channel information, your video list and their statistics (views, likes, comments, subscribers), and uploads or updates videos you have approved.

How data is stored

The app has no server of its own. Your access token and the statistics it downloads are stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party except Google, which receives the API requests.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time on Google Account permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

The app is used by the channel owner, an adult. It does not collect data from viewers or children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
